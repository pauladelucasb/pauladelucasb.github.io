My name is Paula de Lucas, and I am a PhD student in Spanish Linguistics at Georgetown University. 

My research interests focus on <h>mobility contexts</h>, whether temporary, such as study abroad, or indefinite, such as immigration. I wonder how mobility affects <h>second language acquisition and second dialect acquisition</h>.

Moreover, as a Spanish Instructor and a language learner, I ask myself questions that my students often do, such as "How can I become more fluent?" and "Why some people sound as natives and other not?". 

In consequence, I investigate:

<p>I.  The development of <h>second language oral fluency in study-abroad contexts</h>, examining hesitation as a processing phenomenon to better understand the benefits of this learning context.</p>

<p>II. <h>Second dialect acquisition in mobility contexts</h> through the lens of perception and its relationship to language attitudes and social networks. Through this approach, I aim to understand the factors that help or hinder second dialect acquisition.</p>
