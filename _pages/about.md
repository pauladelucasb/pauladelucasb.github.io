My name is Paula de Lucas, and I am a **PhD student in Spanish Linguistics at Georgetown University.**

As a Spaniard living in the US, my research interests focus on **mobility contexts,** whether temporary, such as study abroad, or indefinite, such as immigration. I wonder how mobility, linguistic factors and individual differences favor or hinder second language acquisition and second dialect acquisition.
